Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview & Data Processing

At DevEncoder Suite, accessible from our web application, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. All data transformation utilities (including Base64, Hex, Binary, and URL encoding/decoding) run entirely on the client side using JavaScript inside your web browser. We do not transmit, log, store, or process your input text or data on remote servers.

2. Log Files

DevEncoder Suite follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information.

3. Cookies & Advertising Partners

Like any other website, DevEncoder Suite uses 'cookies' to store information including visitors' preferences and page views. This information is used to optimize user experience.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings (https://www.google.com/settings/ads).

4. GDPR & CCPA Data Rights

We do not collect personal data directly. However, under data protection frameworks like GDPR and CCPA, users have rights regarding third-party cookie tracking. You can manage cookie preferences directly through your browser settings or opt out of third-party vendor cookies by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at contact@yourdomain.com.
